# Cover letter template

**When:** only if the posting asks for one, or the user asks. Keep it to one page and 250–350 words. The same truth rule applies: library facts only.

```
{Full name}
{City} | {Phone} | {Email}

{Date}

{Hiring Manager / Hiring Team}
{Company}

Dear {Name or "Hiring Team"},

{Paragraph 1: hook. The role, and one specific reason this company or product interests you (from the research). One or two sentences.}

{Paragraph 2: proof. The two or three must-haves you match best, each as a short story with a number: what the situation was, what you did, the result. Mirror the JD's terms.}

{Paragraph 3: fit for this team. What you'd bring in the first months, tied to what the JD says they're building (e.g. "build quality engineering from scratch": "I set up QA from scratch for a real-time payments platform..."). Address the biggest gap honestly if it's likely to come up ("I haven't used k6 in production; my load-testing work has been in JMeter, and...").}

{Paragraph 4: close. Availability, work rights or relocation if relevant, and a thank-you.}

Kind regards,
{Full name}
```

**Don't:**
- repeat the resume line by line;
- use "I am writing to apply for...", "passionate", or "team player";
- claim enthusiasm for a product detail you didn't actually read in the research.
