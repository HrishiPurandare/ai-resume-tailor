# Outreach templates

Use these for messages to recruiters and hiring teams. Rules for every message:
- **Facts:** only from the resume library and the real job details. Use the user's real location, notice period and work rights. Never promise "immediate" if they have notice to serve.
- **Lead with the 2–3 strongest matches for this job,** each with a number.
- **Length:** short. Recruiters skim.
- **Never send.** Give the user ready-to-paste text; they send it.

---

## LinkedIn connection note (300 characters max)

```
Hi {First name}, I saw the {Role} role at {Company}. I'm a {location}-based {identity} with {N}+ years in {top skill 1} and {top skill 2}, currently {current role/employer}. {Availability or work-rights line}. Happy to share my CV. Thanks!
```

Count the characters before giving it to the user. LinkedIn cuts notes at 300.

## LinkedIn message or InMail to a recruiter

```
Subject: {Role} ({City}): {2–3 headline skills}

Hi {First name},

I saw your {Role} opening{ for {Client/Company}} and it's a close match to what I do now.

I'm a {current title} at {current employer} in {city}{, where I {scope: lead a team of N / own X}}. Relevant to the role:
- {Must-have 1}: {evidence with number}
- {Must-have 2}: {evidence with number}
- {Must-have 3 or differentiator}: {evidence}

{Location / work rights / notice line, e.g. "I'm already UAE-based, so no relocation is needed, and my notice period is 30 days."}
{One question, e.g. "Could you share who the client is and the rate range?"} I'd be glad to send my CV.

Best regards,
{Full name}
{Phone}
```

## Reply to a recruiter who contacted you

```
Hi {First name},

Thanks for reaching out about the {Role} role. It sounds like a good fit: {one line linking their main need to your strongest match}.

A few quick questions before we go further:
- {Client name / team}
- {Rate or salary range; for contracts: day rate vs monthly, all-inclusive or not, who sponsors the visa}
- {Interview process and start date}

{Availability: notice period, current location.} {If asked for salary: "My expectation is {range}, depending on the full package." Use the report's salary section.}

My CV is attached.

Best regards,
{Full name}
```

## Application email (when the posting says "send your CV to ...")

```
Subject: Application: {Role}, {Full name}

Hi {Name or "Hiring Team"},

I'm applying for the {Role} position{ at {Company}}. My CV is attached.

I'm a {identity} with {N}+ years of experience, {X} of them in {domain the job needs}. {One sentence with 2–3 must-have matches.} {Location and notice period.}

I'd be glad to discuss the role.

Best regards,
{Full name}
{Phone}
```

## "Message to the hiring team" box on an application form

Two or three sentences:
1. Why this company or product specifically. Use a real detail from the research, not flattery.
2. The single strongest match, with a number.
3. Availability or work rights, if they're a likely concern.

## Following up (7–10 days after applying, once)

```
Hi {Name}, I applied for the {Role} role on {date} and wanted to check whether there's an update on the process. I'm still very interested, especially in {one specific aspect of the role}. Thanks for your time.
```
