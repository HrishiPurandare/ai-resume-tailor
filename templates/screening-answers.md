# Screening-form answers

Application forms often ask free-text questions. Answer from the library only, in the user's voice: first person, plain, specific.

## How to answer any question
1. **Answer the question in the first sentence.** ("Yes, for 3 years at {Employer}.")
2. **Add one concrete example with a number.**
3. **Stop.** Most boxes are read in seconds. Aim for 50–120 words unless the form says otherwise, and respect its character limit.

## Common questions

**"Describe your experience with {tool/skill}."**
> I've used {skill} for {duration} at {Employer}, mainly to {what}. For example, I {action} which {result with number}. {Optional: a second context, e.g. personal projects.}

If the skill is confirmed **NOT held**, say so honestly and point to the closest real experience: "I haven't used {X} in production. The closest is {Y} at {Employer}, where I {...}."

**"Describe your experience using AI / GenAI, personally or professionally."**
> Professionally: {AI testing or AI-assisted work from the library, with a result}. Personally: {personal projects, tools used and what was built}. {One line on how they use it responsibly, e.g. reviewing generated code and keeping secrets out of prompts.}

**"Why do you want to work at {Company}?"**
> One real detail from the research (product, market, mission), plus how the user's experience fits it. No flattery.

**"Do you require visa sponsorship?" / "Do you have the right to work in {country}?"**
> Answer **truthfully**, using the work-rights facts in the master resume or experience bank. If sponsorship is needed, say so plainly, and mention any eligibility that helps (e.g. "eligible for the Critical Skills Employment Permit").

**"Salary expectations?"**
> Give a range from `playbooks/salary-research.md`, in the job's currency, stating whether it's annual or monthly and whether it includes benefits. If the user prefers, say "open to discussing based on the full package".

**"Notice period / earliest start date?"**
> The user's real notice period from the library. Never "immediately" unless that's true.

**"Anything else you'd like us to know?"**
> One differentiator the resume doesn't make obvious (an award, a from-scratch build, a domain match) and availability.
